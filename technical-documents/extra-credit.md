# WolfCafe Extra Credit

Document every extra credit option your team completed, so the teaching staff can find it and grade it without searching your repository.  **Extra credit that is not documented here will not be graded.**  If you completed no extra credit, replace this TODO with "No extra credit completed."

Extra credit is listed under Optional Elements in the Team Project problem statement on the course website, and is capped at 90 points.  Let the teaching staff know what you plan to do with the extra credit form linked from the Team Project pages.  Options that must be proposed first (refactoring, new functionality, UI enhancements, and badges other than coverage) must have teaching staff approval before you build them.

Make changes to this page on a document branch (for example, `docs-extra-credit`) or on the feature branch that implements the extra credit, following the [Technical Document Branches](github-process.md#technical-document-branches) process.  Only work merged to `main` by the final deadline is graded.
